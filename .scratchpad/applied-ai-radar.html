Reading this

Where to spend the first applications

Sarvam AI’s Forward Deployed Software Engineer role is the cleanest match to this profile — MCP, multi-agent memory, and evaluation pipelines are named outright, not implied. Innovaccer’s SDE‑I (AI) and Meesho’s Forward Deployed Engineer II sit in the same band with real RAG/agent-framework overlap, though Innovaccer’s live status and stated Master’s requirement are worth confirming directly before investing time.

Everything in the stretch tier is a real, live, on-skill posting that simply asks for more years than the profile has. Forward-deployed and “AI-native” titles in particular tend to weigh what’s been shipped over tenure on paper — Flipkart, Swiggy, Freshworks, and Razorpay’s two roles are all worth applying to rather than filtering out on the years line.

Seventeen companies produced nothing to apply to today, for reasons that range from a confirmed hiring freeze (Krutrim) to career pages that simply don’t render for an automated check (Zomato/Eternal, Yellow.ai, Zepto, Hasura/PromptQL, Slice, Darwinbox, Gupshup) — those seven are worth a direct manual look before writing the company off.

Method & caveats

  • Research conducted mid-August 2026 against each company’s own careers page, plus LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, Glassdoor, and job-board mirrors (BuiltIn, Jobright, WhatJobs, Kula).
  • Naukri, Instahyre, and Wellfound returned HTTP 403 to automated access for nearly every company checked — those three platforms remain effectively unchecked across this entire report, not only for rows marked “unverified.”
  • Highest-confidence rows are the ones pulled directly from a live ATS API — Greenhouse (Razorpay, PhonePe, Postman), Lever (CRED, Meesho), Ashby (Atlan), Workday (Sprinklr, BrowserStack), or SmartRecruiters (Swiggy, Freshworks) — where the entire open-role list was enumerated, not sampled.
  • Postings a source explicitly marks as removed or expired are reported for context only and are never counted as currently open.
  • No posting or URL in this report was invented. Every “none found” or “unverified” row states plainly what was checked and what could not be reached, rather than presenting an access failure as a confirmed absence.

Compiled from six parallel research passes across 27 companies, cross-checked against live ATS APIs where available. Quotes are reproduced verbatim from source postings; anything paraphrased is marked as such.
